How this works
  • Every 15 minutes (GitHub's free cloud, so your PC can be off) the bot downloads real prices for BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE and ADA and lets the fast model decide.
  • The fast model learns from 3 years of real market data what happens after each situation, and retrains itself every 12 hours on the newest outcomes. It is tested on data it never saw. It only counts as having an "edge" if its picks beat simply buying every time.
  • Main account trades only while that edge is proven. Practice account trades every signal so the AI keeps practising and its real skill is measured live.
  • The brain is an LLM (your local mimo2.6 when your PC is on, a small free model in the cloud otherwise). It reviews results, writes lessons into its memory, and can lower risk or skip coins.
  • Learning from mistakes: after every retrain it compares its losing and winning signals, finds what the losers had in common, and turns that into candidate rules.
  • Learning from the internet: once a day the brain reads new trading research (arXiv), crypto news (CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, Decrypt), trading lessons (Babypips) and the Fear & Greed index, and proposes rules.
  • The judge: a rule is used only if it raises the profit per trade on data the model never saw, including the most recent months. It is re-checked after every retrain and retired when it stops working.
  • CLM (your Contrastive Language Model) gives an independent second opinion every 4 hours while the PC is on. Its accuracy is tracked here before anyone trusts it.
  • Costs are simulated: 0.1% fee + 0.05% slippage per trade, stop-loss, take-profit and a time limit on every position.
